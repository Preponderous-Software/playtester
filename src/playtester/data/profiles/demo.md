The title screen waits for Space (or a click). In the world, seen from
above: A and D turn, W walks forward, E chops a tree within reach (1.5
units), Escape pauses. The white square is you; its line shows where you
face. Chop three trees to complete **Gather wood**.

`pt walkto tree` walks to the nearest tree and stops within reach; then
press `e`.
