The game starts in the valley, in first person beside your house, at about
half past four on the first afternoon. A greeting under the crosshair says
where your door is; the HUD (top left) shows the date and time, the body's
needs, the lamp, your standing, the **NEXT** line (what to do now) and the
day's chores; the controls are along the bottom (F1 hides or shows them).
There is no title screen and no probe: everything you know comes from
screenshots.

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Click once in the window (`pt click 640 360`), then the mouse (`pt look DX DY`) | look: about 7 pixels a degree, + right / + down (`pt look 630 0` turns about 90 degrees right). The first click takes the mouse; Esc frees it |
| W A S D | walk |
| Shift (`Shift_L`) | hurry |
| E | use what is here: fill the jar at the ghat, give the water, dig or leave clay, drink at the well, eat at home, sleep (at your door, at night) |
| F | light the lamp, or put it out |
| 1 2 3 4 | at the potter's wheel: throw a water jar, a bowl, a cup or a lamp, then type its name (`pt type "Ganga water"`) and press Enter (`pt key Return`) |
| B | break the pot you are looking at |
| C | the clay lens (after three pots) |
| Tab | the codex: Up and Down choose a page; Tab or Esc goes back. The game pauses while it is open |
| V | in the after-mode (the morning after both teachings): step out and watch the avatar (W A S D and the mouse fly), V again steps back in |
| M | sound on or off (there is no sound device here; that is expected) |
| F1 | show or hide the controls |
| F5 / F9 | save now / load the last save (it also autosaves every two minutes) |
| Esc | free the mouse (**Esc again quits the game: avoid it**) |

Practical notes:

- Steer by screenshots: turn with `pt look`, walk with `pt hold w 1.5`, then
  `pt shot` and look again. Names of houses and places show over them as you
  come near: your door is indigo, the potter's ochre, the shrine's saffron
  under a saffron pennant.
- Time passes at about one game minute a second of play (slower when the
  machine is busy). The sun sets just after six; dusk, when the snakes lie
  on the path between the village and the ghat, is from about a quarter past
  six. Sleeping brings half past six the next morning.
- While a pot is being named, keys type letters (and W A S D do not walk):
  finish the name with Enter.
- Near a snake a fear meter rises and you cannot bring yourself to walk up
  to it. Light the lamp, go as close as you dare, aim the crosshair at the
  snake and hold still for a few seconds (`pt wait 3`), then look again.
- The codex (Tab) gains a page as each teaching happens; one page is open
  from the start.
- There is no combat, no score and no way to lose.
